This entire pricing-style container must be removed from the user-facing application.

Remove:

* "Free forever" badge
* "$0" pricing display
* "always" pricing text
* The entire pricing card/container

* Any unnecessary "Create an invoice" CTA that exists only inside this pricing container

* "100% Free"
* "Free Forever"
* "$0"
* "No credit card"
* "Unlimited free"
* Pricing badges
* Pricing cards
* Subscription plans
* Billing plans

The application should simply function as a free product without repeatedly advertising its price.

The absence of a pricing section should communicate the product's simplicity.

IMPORTANT:

Do not remove actual invoice functionality just because it was listed inside the pricing card.

For example, if the application genuinely supports:

* Invoice creation
* Templates
