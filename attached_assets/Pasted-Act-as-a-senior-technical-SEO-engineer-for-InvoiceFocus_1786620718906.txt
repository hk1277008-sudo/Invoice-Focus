STEP 2 — FIX DUPLICATE TITLES

Every important public indexable page must have a unique, search-intent-specific title.

Do NOT simply append random words to make titles technically unique.

Titles must accurately describe the page.

Examples:

/invoice-generator
Free Invoice Generator — Create Professional Invoices | InvoiceFocus

/invoice-template
Free Invoice Template — Professional Invoice Templates | InvoiceFocus

/quote
Free Quote Generator — Create Professional Quotes | InvoiceFocus

/estimate

/receipt
Free Receipt Generator — Create Professional Receipts | InvoiceFocus

/credit-note
Free Credit Note Generator — Create Credit Notes | InvoiceFocus

/purchase-order
Free Purchase Order Template | InvoiceFocus

Use appropriate titles for the actual routes that exist.

Do not invent routes just to solve duplicate metadata.

STEP 3 — FIX DUPLICATE META DESCRIPTIONS

Every important public indexable page should have a unique meta description.

Descriptions should:

- accurately describe the page
- naturally contain the primary search intent
- be useful to a searcher
- avoid keyword stuffing
- avoid repetitive wording
- normally target approximately 140–160 characters where practical
- not make unsupported claims

Do not use one global homepage description across every page.

STEP 4 — REVIEW SEO ROUTES

Inspect seoroute.tsx and all related SEO route configuration.

Separate pages into:

A. Keep and index
Pages with meaningful unique search intent and useful content.

B. Keep but noindex
Pages that are useful for navigation/product functionality but should not compete in organic search.

C. Remove
Only pages that are genuinely redundant, obsolete, broken, or duplicate another page with no independent search intent.

Do NOT remove useful SEO landing pages simply because they are not receiving traffic yet.

STEP 5 — CANONICALS

Make sure every public SEO page has a self-referencing canonical URL unless there is a legitimate reason to canonicalize elsewhere.

Canonical URLs must use:

https://invoicefocus.com/...

Never use:

- localhost
- preview domains
- Vercel domains
- Replit domains
- duplicate URL variants
