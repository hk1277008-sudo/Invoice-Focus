WRITING QUALITY
==================================================

Write for real people first.

The articles must feel:
- natural
- clear
- specific
- useful
- conversational but professional
- confident without sounding corporate
- original
- practical

Avoid generic AI-style writing.

DO NOT:
- use repetitive introductions
- use predictable "In today's fast-paced world" language
- stuff keywords into every paragraph
- repeat the same point using different wording
- create artificial statistics
- invent expert opinions
- invent customer experiences
- invent surveys
- invent company claims
- copy competitor wording
- paraphrase competitor articles sentence-by-sentence
- create content purely to target search engines

Use natural variation in sentence length and structure.

Where appropriate, include:
- practical examples
- realistic scenarios
- simple explanations
- decision tables
- checklists

- useful examples

The reader should finish the article feeling that they learned something useful.

Google's current guidance emphasizes original, helpful, people-first content rather than content created primarily to attract search traffic, so prioritize actual usefulness and first-hand/product-relevant insight over keyword volume.
